1. What the year did

DASS production is a small, always-on Sydney account: one CouchDB EC2, one Fargate conductor service, a shared ALB, CloudWatch, and a CSIRO security baseline (Security Hub, GuardDuty, Inspector, Config). There is also a separate Uptime Kuma t3.micro (~$10.76/month). There are no Reserved Instances and no Savings Plans.

Monthly unblended cost

Source: AWS Cost Explorer · UnblendedCost · monthly · 1 Nov 2025 – 25 Nov 2026. November 2026 is estimated month-to-date; the dashed marker is the CE mean forecast for the full month ($411.89).

The shape in one paragraph

Spend sat around $284.07–$291.77 at the end of 2025, then stepped up twice: CouchDB was resized from m5.large → m5.xlarge in mid-January 2026 (and its data volume from ~150 GB to 300 GB), and Fargate was doubled from 2 vCPU / 4 GB to 4 vCPU / 8 GB through February–March. The plateau of ~$523/month held from March to August. On about 21 September 2026 Fargate returned to 2 vCPU, and October landed at $422.21. November is tracking toward ~$412.

Month-on-month, the only structurally large moves are those two capacity changes. Data-transfer and ALB LCU stay in the noise (a few dollars). First-of-month daily spikes in the 90-day view are billing cadence, not incidents.

Takeaway. This account does not have a usage-driven cost problem. It has a capacity configuration problem. Almost all of the +$239.37 rise from Nov 2025 to the July peak is CouchDB instance/volume size and Fargate task size, plus the GST that rides on them. Scale the compute back (or cover it with a Compute Savings Plan) and the bill follows.

No Reserved Instance on the CouchDB host. A 1-year standard RI on m5.xlarge (or m5.large if downsized) is the instance-family alternative to a Compute SP. typically 20–30% vs on-demand if size is stable A Compute SP is more flexible because it also covers Fargate. Prefer SP unless CouchDB is the only long-lived EC2 you will keep.

Combining CouchDB downsize and the quoted $0.243/h SP would double-count. A realistic bundle, if CouchDB really can return to m5.large and Fargate stays at 2.25 vCPU, is roughly $65 instance + a smaller SP on the remaining Fargate/Kuma, landing the account closer to the late-2025 $284.07–$291.77 band plus GST and the extra CloudWatch/secrets that did not exist then — call it ~$320–360/month rather than today’s ~$412 forecast.

Things that are fine

  • ALB LCU is tiny. Do not spend engineering time on request-path efficiency for cost.
  • Data transfer out is $6.27 over 13 months. Not a lever.
  • S3, CloudFront, Lambda, Glue, KMS, Step Functions are cents.
  • Cost anomalies since 27 Aug are noise: CloudWatch +$0.39, Security Hub +$0.15, S3 +$0.17, GuardDuty cents. The account name on those events is BSS-PROD-grmp.
  • Daily AWS Backup of the 300 GB volume is cheap; the disk itself is the cost.
